# Demo script (about 5 minutes)

Topic for the demo: **Common denominators** (or any topic your audience can follow in 30 seconds).

## Before you go on stage
- [ ] Open `<server>/health` (wakes a sleeping free server)
- [ ] Teacher laptop on the projector, full screen; **Hide names** ticked
- [ ] Two team members' phones already joined as backup "students" (they can tap colors on cue)
- [ ] Record a **backup video** of the full flow; know where the file is
- [ ] Test on the venue wifi. If it dies, play the video

## Script

| Time | Say | Do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | "A teacher explains for 30 minutes. Who got lost? They usually find out on the exam, weeks later. Students rarely raise a hand." | Problem slide with one real statistic (see `docs/research.md`) |
| 0:30 | "Class Pulse tells the teacher, live, without anyone speaking up." | Start class on the projector, join code on screen |
| 0:45 | "Judges, join with your phone." | Judges join. Student count climbs live |
| 1:15 | "I'll teach something for 20 seconds. You tap how well you follow." | Type the topic, tap **Check in now**. Teach one confusing minute out loud, quickly |
| 1:50 | "Tap any time. Green, yellow or red." | The % moves live, the timeline starts drawing |
| 2:20 | "The teacher sees the class is at 38 percent, and the advice: slow down. Not who is lost, how much of the class is lost." | Point at the big number and the advice line |
| 2:45 | "And what would help: most say they need an example." | Point at "What would help" |
| 3:00 | "So the teacher re-explains with an example... and checks in again on the same topic." | Re-explain in 20 seconds, tap **Check in now** with the same topic |
| 3:30 | "Colors cleared. Everyone re-marks. Watch the number." | Before/after: 38% to 88%. Say the numbers out loud |
| 4:00 | "The timeline shows exactly where in the lesson we lost them, and where we won them back." | Point at the dip and the recovery |
| 4:20 | "Focus mode tells the teacher if a student leaves the page." | Switch tab on one phone, the alert appears |
| 4:40 | "Teachers stop finding out on the exam. They find out while they can still fix it." | Vision line, thank you |

## Tips
- Make the first minute confusing on purpose (fast, no example) so the % drops. Then the re-teach with an example makes the recovery obvious.
- If judges all tap green, use your backup phones to tap yellow and red on cue so the dashboard has something to show.
- Be upfront about the limit: it is self-reported, a signal for the teacher to check verbally, not a test. Judges respect that.
- Practice the whole thing 3 times, with a timer, on the real projector setup.
