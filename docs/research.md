## 1. The problem (find 2 to 3 sources)

Search leads (check what the research actually says before using any of it):
- **Students rarely say they are lost.** Look for research on why students do not ask questions or raise their hand in class (fear of judgment, social pressure).
- **Formative assessment / feedback during teaching.** Look for the classic work on formative assessment and on "checking for understanding" during a lesson.
- **Low-tech versions of this idea.** "Traffic light" self-assessment cards in classrooms, and clicker / student response systems. These show teachers already want this signal.
- **Can students judge their own understanding?** Look for research on self-assessment accuracy and the "illusion of knowing". This matters: our signal is self-reported, so we should know its limits and say so.

## 4. Limitations we will state honestly

- The signal is self-reported: students can over- or under-report. It tells the teacher when to check, it does not prove understanding.
- A percentage over the students who marked ignores those who did not. The app shows "Not marked" so this stays visible.
- Focus mode only reports leaving the page.
